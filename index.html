Erun

Errands, Handled.

A vetted, professional errand-running service — one app, one wallet, one runner, multiple stops. This is the working customer app, not a slideshow: every figure on screen is calculated, and the wallet really settles.

Try it yourself

  1. Book an errand. Pick a service, search a shop, then drag the item budget up or down — the pricing recalculates against it live.
  2. Watch the spend cap work. Approve the item mid-errand, then read the receipt: the runner never spends the cap, and the difference is swept straight back.
  3. Check the wallet. The money is held while the errand runs and settles when it finishes. Cancel instead and it refunds to the cent.

Open this page on your phone and add it to your home screen — it installs and runs offline.
